More proof.

Most of my process is the same regardless of medium: understand the constraint, find the smallest version of the real problem, then build something someone can react to.

The five case studies on the homepage have the clearest before-and-after, but they're not the whole list. A data-pipeline testing tool at Airbnb. Accessibility standards at Kaiser Permanente, including getting Ghirardelli Square to full ADA compliance. A unified UX workflow at Southwest Airlines. Early heuristic work on a low-vision AR headset at CitrusBits. Different companies, different problems — same instinct.

I sketch fast, prototype faster, and would rather ship something rough and learn from it than polish a guess.
