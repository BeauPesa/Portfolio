Components

Hover the samples. Every interactive circle on the site shares one size, the nav button’s: 30px on desktop, 26px on phones.

Buttons

Height = nav button (30 / 26). Width spans pinstripe 4 → 6 on case cards (two columns); 2 columns on phones. Radius fully round. Hover fills over 0.25s.

Badges

60px circle in the case ink, white type. Hover inverts (white fill, ink type), adds a 4px ring at 30% ink, and scales slightly. Phones: the badge straddles the card’s top-left corner, above everything.

Navigation states

Desktop: icon nav down the left on pinstripe 1, socials at its foot, section dots on the right. Phones: icon nav in a bottom pill, dots in a top pill, socials at the left middle. Page to page, the lit button and the dots pill animate across.

Dashed rule

Case cards frame the title and role with a pair, in the case’s rule colour; the NDA veil uses a thinner, 1.5× longer pair.

Grid, spacing & motion

Seven dashed pinstripes; gutters half a nav button either side; one spacing unit, the nav button.
